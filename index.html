How scoring works
  • Liftoff (0–1,000): up to 750 for reaching the day's par number of answers, plus 50 for each answer nobody else in the crew gave (max 5). It keeps updating as the rest of the crew plays, so check back later.
  • Signal Lock (0–1,000): a satellite downlinks a new clue about the mystery thing every orbit (9 seconds). Solve it on clue 1 for 1,000, then 800, 600, 400 or 250. Each wrong guess costs 75. One extra orbit after clue 5, then the signal is lost and you score 0.
  • Landing (0–1,000): three estimates worth up to 333 each. Exact is full points, 2× off keeps about 70%, and 10× off scores zero. Too high is redshifted, too low is blueshifted.
  • Season: daily totals add up into a race from Earth to Pluto (60,000 points). A new season starts every month.
